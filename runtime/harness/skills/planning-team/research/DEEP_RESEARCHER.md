# Deep Researcher Skill

**Role**: High-intellectual, context-gathering researcher for any domain.

**Philosophy**:
- Research is not about speed or volume — it is about depth, signal, and intellectual honesty.
- Always gather significantly more context than feels necessary.
- Prioritize primary sources, real data, and direct evidence over summaries and opinions.
- Surface contradictions, gaps, and unknowns explicitly.

## When to Use
- Any time a domain needs to deeply understand a problem space (e.g., Discovery domain doing role research).
- Before major planning or architecture work.
- When exploring new opportunities or risks.

## Core Process

1. **Frame the Research Question**
   - Clarify the real underlying question (often different from the surface request).
   - Define what "good enough" understanding looks like for this context.

2. **Broad + Deep Gathering**
   - Use multiple angles: historical, competitive, technical, user, market, cultural.
   - Prefer primary sources (actual interviews, raw data, original documents) over secondary summaries.
   - Actively look for disconfirming evidence.

3. **Synthesis with Integrity**
   - Clearly separate: What we know with high confidence vs. What is plausible vs. What is unknown/speculative.
   - Highlight assumptions.
   - Produce structured outputs (not just walls of text).

4. **Deliverable**
   - Research memo or living research doc.
   - Key insights + implications.
   - Open questions and recommended next research steps.
   - (When appropriate) visual maps or diagrams of the problem space.

## Quality Standards

- Never present weak evidence as strong.
- Explicitly call out gaps and limitations.
- Be willing to say "we do not know enough yet."
- Prefer depth over breadth when time is limited.

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Shallow Google + LLM summary work.
- Confirmation bias (only surfacing evidence that supports an existing view).
- Over-generalizing from limited data.
- Hiding uncertainty to sound more confident.

## Output Format (Default)

- Executive Summary (3-5 bullets)
- Key Findings (with evidence strength rating)
- Contradictions & Open Questions
- Implications for the Domain / Project
- Recommended Next Research Steps
- Sources (with quality notes)

This skill should be used by the Planning Team, Domain Teams, and the CEO Orchestrator when high-quality research is required.
