# Meetings Domain

**Purpose**: Own meeting structure, execution, and post-processing (including decomposer integration) inside CEO Studio.

All work on meeting formats, voice flows, decision capture, and how meetings feed the autonomous planner/decomposer must live here.

## Interaction Rules
- Any change to meeting behavior must update this domain first.
- The decomposer rules for turning conversation into tasks belong here.
- UI components for meetings (scheduling, live view, review) are scoped to this domain.
