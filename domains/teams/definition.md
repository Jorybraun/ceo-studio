## Purpose / Ownership
Own how CEO Studio groups agents, humans, roles, rooms, capabilities, and routing relationships so work can be assigned, discussed, convened, and reviewed by the right participants.

## Overarching Goal / Long-term Outcome
CEO Studio can explain, manage, and use teams as operational work units: who belongs to a team, what the team can do, which domains it serves, which room it uses, and when the CEO should route work or meetings to that team.

## Boundaries / Ownership
- Owns team definitions and team membership.
- Owns team capability coverage and gaps.
- Owns domain-to-team routing intent.
- Owns team room conventions and meeting roster selection.
- Does not own individual provider credentials or model funding.
- Does not own Kanban storage itself.
- Does not own domain definitions outside team relationships.

## Key Capabilities / Initial Features
- Team registry UI and editing workflow.
- Team membership management.
- Team capability matrix.
- Domain-to-team routing recommendations.
- Meeting team selection and room handoff.
- Dispatch team recommendation before agent launch.
- Team health and coverage review.
- Subdomain candidate modeling for Membership, Rooms, Dispatch Routing, and Capability Coverage.
